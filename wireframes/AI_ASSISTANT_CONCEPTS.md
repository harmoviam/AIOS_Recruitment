### Candidate Quality Score
```

│  ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5 stars)    │

│  Salary Fit: 8.2      │
│                        │
│  🔍 Analyze Full Score │
└────────────────────────┘
```

### What Score Means
- **9-10**: Exceptional fit, prioritize immediately
- **8-9**: Very strong match, schedule soon
- **7-8**: Good match, continue pipeline
- **6-7**: Potential match, get more info
- **<6**: Weak fit, consider rejecting

### Score Factors Explained
- **Skills Match**: Percentage of required skills present
- **Experience**: Years aligned with role requirements
- **Communication**: WhatsApp/email response quality
- **Availability**: Readiness to join timeline
- **Salary Fit**: Expectation vs. budget alignment

## 11. AI GUARDRAILS & LIMITATIONS

### What AI Won't Do
```
❌ Make final hiring decisions
❌ Access candidate's personal WhatsApp
❌ Override recruiter preferences
❌ Schedule without confirmation
❌ Make unilateral rejections
```

### Recruiter Always in Control
```
✅ AI suggests → Recruiter decides
✅ AI recommends → Recruiter acts
✅ AI alerts → Recruiter responds
✅ AI predicts → Recruiter validates
✅ AI learns → Recruiter corrects
```
